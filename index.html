The majors

What each degree requires on its own, before adding a minor.

Marketing

BS in Business Administration · Barney School
Total
120 credits, including gen ed, the business core, and the major.
Business core
World of Business, Intro to Quantitative Methods for Business, Financial and Managerial Accounting, Management and Organizational Behavior, Business and Professional Communication, Exploring and Planning Career Options.
Required marketing
Consumer Behavior, International Marketing, Marketing Research, Marketing Strategy (capstone), Marketing Internship.
Electives
Sales Management, Marketing Communications, Digital Marketing, Digital Marketing Analytics, Marketing Analytics.
Best for
Business-side roles: brand management, market research, sales, marketing strategy.

Communication

BA · College of Arts and Sciences
Total
120 credits; Advertising and PR or Media Strategies emphasis is 45 credits.
Emphases
Advertising and Public Relations, Media Strategies, or Communication Studies.
Example courses
Communication and Advertising, Introduction to Public Relations, The Agency: Advertising Copywriting and Layout.
Hands-on
Campaigns for real clients, Crested Communications (student-run agency), national AAF and PRSSA competitions.
Best for
Agency work, advertising, PR, social media, and media strategy.

English

BA · College of Arts and Sciences
Total
120 credits; the major is 36, leaving about 26 credits of free electives.
Core (18)
Two intro literature courses, sophomore learning community, internship, senior capstone, literature elective.
Creative Writing emphasis (18)
One professional writing or grammar course, four workshops (poetry, fiction, creative nonfiction, playwriting), literature elective.
Literature emphasis (18)
One professional writing or grammar course, one modern language course, five 300/400-level literature courses.
Best for
Writing, editing, publishing, content, and copywriting careers.
